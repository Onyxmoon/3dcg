> This folder contains the Processing Sketch and other documents for Task Sheet 1 in the 3D Computer Graphics Lab.

## Task

> For your free work interpretation, choose templates from an artist who created non-object-related works of art (abstract art).
>
> Create a digital variation of the artwork and test the design rules of the image composition in a processing sketch. Your digital image should adapt to different window sizes and always take up the entire window area. However, no animations or interactions have to be implemented yet.
>
> Use the graphical primitives defined by Processing (point, rectangle, ellipse) or use beginShape and endShape to draw freely defined shapes. It is recommended to use flexible transformations directly in the image composition instead of working with absolute coordinates.
>
> The goal of the task is not the exact reproduction of a work of art - for this purpose a good photograph of the work of art itself is sufficient. Strives for a free interpretation of the design rules under as variable (image size, aspect ratio).
>
> Always compares the variation created by the Processing Sketch with the work templates. Your result will be perfect if the variation created by your Processing Sketch has a similar aesthetic appearance as the artwork templates.



## Realization

The implementation occured with [Processing](https://processing.org/) 3.5.4.
